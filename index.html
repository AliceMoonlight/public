How to use this board

Match strengths: STRONG = prompt and dark/horror connection explicitly evidenced; MEDIUM = one side requires a close semantic or lyrical interpretation; WEAK = promising lead with a tenuous Halloween connection. All entries require listening and Human approval. Genre or artist names alone do not prove the song has a Halloween theme.

Lyric availability: “Lyrics on source” means Bandcamp publicly displays the song words. To respect artists’ copyrights, this board stores concise summaries rather than full lyric transcriptions. Use “Read lyrics” to see the source.

In-page audio: Bandcamp track pages offer Share / Embed. Copy its full iframe code, click “Add player” on the song card, and paste it. The embedded player will appear in this browser and be saved locally. Players may be subject to artist permissions or browser settings. Embeds are not preconfigured because reliable track IDs were unavailable in the research interface.

Saving: Stars, notes, decisions and embedded-player URLs are stored in this browser’s local storage for this HTML file. They do not upload to a server. Export your reviews often so that changing browsers or clearing site data does not erase your work.

Scope: 31 prompts, no substitutions to existing ALICE.295 provisional choices; Halloween word bank v1.1 excludes Doll and the discarded autumn/uncanny sections. No audio download, mix, publication, license claim, or listening test occurred.
